# Community code of conduct

CandleAudit welcomes people who want to improve reproducible data checks and educational backtesting software.

Be respectful, constructive, patient and inclusive in issues, reviews and discussions. Focus on technical facts and reproducible evidence, not personal criticism. Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam and deliberate exposure of private information are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove abusive material and limit participation where necessary. Please report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's report mechanisms; avoid posting personal or sensitive allegations in public issues.

This community code applies to the project's repository and related project discussions. Contributions remain subject to the project's MIT license and its security reporting policy.
